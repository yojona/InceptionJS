# Trademarks

The name InceptionJS belongs to the project. The code is open source under the
Apache License 2.0, which does not grant rights to the project's names (section 6),
so here is what that means in practice. It is meant to be easy to follow.

You are welcome to:

- Use InceptionJS in your product, your company and your side projects.
- Say that your software is compatible with InceptionJS or "built on InceptionJS",
  and link to this project.
- Write about it, teach it, review it and make videos about it.
- Fork it and publish your fork.

Please do not:

- Distribute a modified version under the name InceptionJS, or under a name that
  could be confused with it. If you fork it or ship a modified distribution, give it
  a different name and say that it is based on InceptionJS.
- Suggest that the project endorses or runs your product or service when it does not.

An unmodified build of this repository's releases may of course keep the name.
If you are unsure whether a use is fine, open an issue and ask.
